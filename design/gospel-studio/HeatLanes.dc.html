Demands to move

Secondary dominants with altered tensions on top. Each one leans hard into the chord a fifth below it.

VI7alt A7alt Pulls into ii
III7alt E7alt The 7-3-6 hinge
V7alt G7alt Hardest pull home
II7alt D7alt Pulls into V

Where these go. Drop one immediately before the chord a fifth below it — A7alt into Dm9, E7alt into Am11. That single move is what separates a worship chart from a gospel one.

Borrowed from elsewhere

Chords lifted out of the parallel minor. They belong to no scale degree here, which is exactly why they land.

♭III E♭maj7 Chromatic mediant
♭VI A♭maj7 Aeolian borrow
♭VII B♭maj7 Mixolydian lift
♭VIIm B♭m7 Subtonic shadow

Where these go. Step down through ♭VII and ♭VI on the way back to the tonic, or slide into ♭III to make a bridge sound like it changed key without ever leaving.
